  1. Getting There

    2.

    You can reach Mombasa from Nairobi in three main ways:

    • By train – the SGR Madaraka Express (about 5 hours)
      • Economy Class
      • First Class
    • By air– 1 hour to Moi International Airport
    • By plane– about 8 hours by bus or car

  2. The 3-Day Itinerary

    3. Day 1: History & Old Town

    Fort Jesus was built by the Portuguese in 1593.

    1. Morning: Visit Fort Jesus
    2. Lunch: Biryani at a local restaurant
    3. Afternoon: Walking tour of Old Town
      • Mombasa Old Port
      • Spice market
      • Swahili Carved Doors
    4. Evening: Sunset dhow cruise
    Day 2: Beach Day

    Diani Beach – voted Africa's leading beach destination.

    1. Snorkelling at the Marine Park
    2. Lunch: Fresh seafood on the beach
    3. Camel ride along the shore
    Day 3: Beach & Relaxation

    Spend a slow day enjoying the coastline, local seafood, and a beachside sunset.

  4. Packing Checklist

    5. Clothing

    Light cotton clothes, swimwear, a hat, sandals

    Health

    Sunscreen (SPF 50), insect repellent, water bottle

    Documents

    National ID or passport, tickets, booking confirmations

  5. Travel Tips

    6. "Pole pole" – slowly, slowly. Life at the coast moves at a relaxed pace, so enjoy it!

    • Learn a few Swahili words: Jambo (Hello), Asante (Thank you), Karibu (Welcome).
    • Best time to visit: July to October and January to March.
    • Always dress modestly in Old Town.

Old Town streets are narrow – perfect for exploring on foot.
